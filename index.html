How to use

  1. Tap Start measuring and allow camera access.
  2. Point at the floor or a table and move the phone slowly side to side until a yellow ring appears.
  3. Put the cross on the start of the object and tap + (or tap anywhere on the screen).
  4. Move to the end point. The length updates live. Tap + again to finish.

For the best accuracy, use good light, keep the phone 30 cm to 2 m from the object, and avoid plain white or shiny surfaces. Expect about ±1–2 cm.
